At Ananta Cloud, our platform is optimized for teams leveraging **OpenTofu** and **Terragrunt**. We ensure compatibility with all versions of OpenTofu and HashiCorp Terraform up to **v1.5.7**, the last version available under the open-source license. Additionally, we offer partial support for other Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools like **Kubernetes**, **Packer**, **Docker**, and **Pulumi**.

Please note that certain modules may leverage features only available in later versions of OpenTofu/Terraform. In these cases, the module will specify a minimum required version to ensure full functionality.

### Do I have to use Terragrunt?

Some of our core platform products—such as **Pipelines** and **Account Factory**—are designed specifically to work with **Terragrunt** and thus require it as part of your IaC workflow.

For other offerings like the **Library**, you have the flexibility to use them with or without Terragrunt. That said, we **strongly recommend using Terragrunt**, especially for customers managing infrastructure at scale. Terragrunt simplifies orchestration and enhances scalability for OpenTofu, and it enables us to provide the most effective support tailored to your implementation.
