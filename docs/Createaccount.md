# Create Your Account

Follow these steps to create and activate your Ananta Cloud Developer Portal account:

---

## 1. Accept Your Invitation

You’ll receive an invitation email from `grunty@anantacloud.io` prompting you to set up your account.

- Click the button in the email to begin.
- Review and agree to the Privacy Policy and Website Terms.
- Use the email address that received the invitation:
  - If it's a Google email, click **Continue with Google**
  - Otherwise, enter your name and click **Continue with Email**

> 💡 The initial invitation email may be forwarded to the designated team admin after subscription purchase.

---

## 2. Confirm Your Email Address

If you chose **Continue with Email**, check your inbox for a sign-in link.

- Ananta Cloud uses a passwordless login system via email link.
- Click the link to complete the login.

> ⚠️ If you see an error that the link has expired, it may be due to security tools (e.g., Microsoft SafeLink™, Mimecast) that pre-process links.  
> Request a new link, and copy-paste it directly into your browser instead of clicking.

> 🔐 Sign-in links expire after 10 minutes. To get a new one, visit [app.anantacloud.io](https://app.anantacloud.io) and enter your email again.

---

## 3. Provide Account Details

If you're the organization admin, you’ll be prompted to fill in:

- Company address
- Phone number
- Billing email

After entering the details, click **Continue** to finalize your account setup.
