As cloud adoption accelerates across industries, enterprises face growing challenges in setting up secure, scalable, and governed cloud environments. Without a structured foundation, organizations risk compliance breaches, cost overruns, and architectural inconsistencies.

**Ananta Cloud,** a specialized cloud consulting firm, addresses this challenge with its **Landing Zone Setup Services**—a suite of standardized, automated, and cloud-agnostic offerings that enable organizations to confidently launch their cloud journeys.

We help customers establish **enterprise-grade Landing Zones** on **AWS, Microsoft Azure,** and **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**—each aligned with **industry frameworks, security best practices,** and **compliance** requirements.

### Security and Compliance Focus

- Security and compliance are at the heart of every Landing Zone:
- Zero Trust architecture baseline
- CIS Benchmarks, NIST 800-53 mappings, ISO 27001 alignment
- Encryption at rest and in transit
- Role-based access control (RBAC / ABAC)
- Secure key management (KMS/Key Vault/Cloud KMS)
- Audit readiness support for SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, FedRAMP
