# Key Capabilities

- **🔐 Security & Vulnerability Scanning** – Automatically scan container images and Helm charts for CVEs and known threats.  
- **📋 Policy Enforcement** – Define retention, replication, and access control policies for secure operations.  
- **🤖 Image Signing & Trust** – Ensure images are signed and verified before deployment to production.  
- **🌐 Multi-Cluster & Replication** – Synchronize images across multiple Trustary instances or cloud regions.  
- **🔗 CI/CD Integration** – Integrate Trustary into pipelines for build, scan, and deploy workflows.
