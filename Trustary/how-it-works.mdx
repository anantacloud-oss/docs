# How Trustary Works

Trustary follows a streamlined workflow to manage container images:

- **📦 Push Images & Charts** – Users push container images or Helm charts to Trustary.  
- **🔍 Vulnerability Scanning** – Trustary scans images using integrated security scanners.  
- **🔐 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** – Ensures proper permissions and access control.  
- **✍️ Image Signing & Verification** – Images are signed and verified for trust and compliance.  
- **📜 Policy Enforcement** – Enforce retention, replication, and promotion workflows.  
- **📊 Audit Logs & Reports** – Gain full visibility across teams and clusters.
