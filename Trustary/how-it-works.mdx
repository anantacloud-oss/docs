## How Trustary Works

1. **Build & Push**  
   Developers or CI pipelines build container images and push them to Trustary.

2. **Scan & Enforce Policies**  
   Images are automatically scanned for vulnerabilities and validated against security policies.

3. **Store & Manage**  
   Images are organized into projects with role-based access control.

4. **Replicate & Distribute**  
   Policy-based replication syncs images across registries for multi-cloud, DR, and global deployments.

5. **Deploy Securely**  
   Only trusted and compliant images are pulled into Kubernetes clusters.
