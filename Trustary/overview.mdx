# Trustary

**Trustary** is a secure, cloud-native container registry built for modern DevOps and DevSecOps workflows.  
It helps teams store, secure, scan, replicate, and govern container images across the entire CI/CD lifecycle.

Designed for high-velocity cloud-native environments, Trustary goes beyond traditional registries by embedding **security, policy enforcement, and enterprise-grade access control** directly into the container supply chain.

---

## What Is Trustary?

Trustary is an enterprise-ready container registry that extends standard Docker registry capabilities with:

- Security-first design  
- Policy-driven governance  
- Identity and access management  
- Compliance and audit readiness  

By placing Trustary close to CI pipelines and Kubernetes clusters, teams gain faster image pulls, improved security, and consistent control across multi-cloud environments.
