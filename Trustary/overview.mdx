# Trustary

**Trustary** is an open-source, cloud-native container registry that securely stores, signs, and scans container images and other artifacts for vulnerabilities. It helps organizations enforce policies, manage access, and maintain trust across development and production environments.

Trustary integrates seamlessly with Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud-native workflows, ensuring container images are **secure, compliant, and readily available** for deployment.

---

## Why Trustary?

Modern containerized environments face several operational and security challenges:

- Uncontrolled distribution of container images  
- Lack of vulnerability scanning in CI/CD pipelines  
- Difficulty enforcing access control and role-based policies  
- Inconsistent image signing and trust verification  
- Limited visibility into container registries across teams and clusters  

Trustary addresses these challenges by providing a **secure, policy-driven, and observable registry** for container artifacts.

---

## How Trustary Works

Trustary follows a streamlined workflow to manage container images:

1. Users push container images or Helm charts to Harbor  
2. Trustary scans images for known vulnerabilities using integrated scanners  
3. Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures proper permissions  
4. Images can be signed and verified for trust and compliance  
5. Policies can enforce retention, replication, and promotion workflows  
6. Audit logs and reports provide full visibility across teams and clusters  

---

## Key Capabilities

### 🔐 Security & Vulnerability Scanning
Automatically scans container images and Helm charts for CVEs and known threats.

### 📋 Policy Enforcement
Define retention, replication, and access control policies for secure operations.

### 🤖 Image Signing & Trust
Ensure images are signed and verified before deployment to production.

### 🌐 Multi-Cluster & Replication
Synchronize images across multiple Trustary instances or cloud regions.

### 🔗 CI/CD Integration
Integrate Trustary seamlessly into pipelines for build, scan, and deploy workflows.
