# Trustary

**Trustary** is an open-source, cloud-native container registry that securely stores, and scans container images and other artifacts for vulnerabilities. It helps organizations enforce policies, manage access, and maintain trust across development and production environments.

Trustary integrates seamlessly with CI/CD pipelines, and cloud-native workflows, ensuring container images are **secure, compliant, and readily available** for deployment.

---

## Why Trustary?

Modern containerized environments face several operational and security challenges:

- Uncontrolled distribution of container images  
- Lack of vulnerability scanning in CI/CD pipelines  
- Difficulty enforcing access control and role-based policies  
- Inconsistent image signing and trust verification  
- Limited visibility into container registries across teams and clusters  

Trustary addresses these challenges by providing a **secure, policy-driven, and observable registry** for container artifacts.
