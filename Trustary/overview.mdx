## What Is Trustary?

**Trustary** is a secure, enterprise-grade container registry designed for modern **cloud-native and DevSecOps environments**.

It acts as a centralized platform to **store, manage, scan, and replicate** container images and Helm charts while enforcing **security and governance policies** across the entire CI/CD pipeline. Unlike traditional registries that focus only on image storage, Trustary embeds **security controls, access management, and compliance checks** directly into the container lifecycle.

Trustary integrates seamlessly with **CI/CD tools, Kubernetes, and multiple external registries**, enabling teams to move fast without compromising security. With built-in **RBAC, vulnerability scanning, SSO support, and policy-driven replication**, Trustary ensures that only trusted and compliant artifacts reach production.

Built for scale and enterprise adoption, Trustary helps DevOps teams implement **DevSecOps best practices** by making container security automated, consistent, and auditable.
