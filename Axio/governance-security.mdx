title: Governance & Security
---

Axio helps organizations maintain governance, security, and operational control across infrastructure deployments.

## Governance Controls

- **Centralized Access Control** — Manage permissions through users, teams, organizations, and roles. This helps ensure that infrastructure access remains controlled, auditable, and aligned with organizational responsibilities.

- **Policy Enforcement** — Apply governance, security, and compliance standards consistently across infrastructure deployments. Policies help reduce risk and ensure infrastructure follows approved operational practices.

- **Approval Workflows** — Require reviews and approvals before critical infrastructure changes are executed. This provides additional oversight and helps prevent unauthorized or unintended deployments.

- **Role-Based Governance** — Assign responsibilities and permissions based on organizational roles. This supports separation of duties and promotes secure infrastructure operations.

- **Organizational Visibility** — Gain a centralized view of infrastructure activities, permissions, policies, and deployments. Improved visibility helps teams manage governance effectively at scale.

## Security & Compliance

- **Credential Security** — Securely store and manage cloud provider credentials within the platform. Centralized credential management reduces exposure of sensitive information and simplifies operations.

- **Identity Integration** — Integrate with enterprise identity providers such as Azure AD and OIDC. Centralized authentication simplifies user management and strengthens access controls.

- **Auditability** — Track deployment history, infrastructure actions, and operational activities from a single interface. Audit records improve accountability and support compliance requirements.
