# Governance & Security

Axio helps organizations maintain security, compliance, and operational control.

## Centralized Access Control

Manage permissions through:

- Users
- Teams
- Organizations
- Roles

## Policy Enforcement

Apply governance standards consistently across deployments.

## Approval Workflows

Require reviews before critical infrastructure changes are executed.

## Credential Security

Store and manage cloud credentials securely within the platform.

## Auditability

Track infrastructure activities and deployment history through a centralized interface.
