title: Governance & Security
---

- **Centralized Access Control** — Manage permissions through users, teams, organizations, and roles. This ensures that access to infrastructure resources is controlled, auditable, and aligned with organizational responsibilities.

- **Policy Enforcement** — Apply governance, security, and compliance standards consistently across infrastructure deployments. Policies help reduce risk and ensure infrastructure follows approved organizational practices.

- **Approval Workflows** — Require reviews and approvals before critical infrastructure changes are executed. This provides additional oversight for sensitive deployments and helps prevent unintended changes.

- **Credential Security** — Store and manage cloud provider credentials securely within the platform. Centralized credential management reduces the exposure of sensitive information while simplifying infrastructure operations.

- **Auditability** — Track infrastructure activities, deployment history, and operational actions through a centralized interface. This improves visibility, accountability, and compliance reporting.

- **Role-Based Governance** — Assign responsibilities and permissions based on organizational roles. This enables separation of duties and helps maintain secure operational practices across teams.

- **Identity Integration** — Integrate with enterprise identity providers such as Azure AD and OIDC-enabled platforms. Centralized authentication simplifies user management and strengthens security controls.

- **Organizational Visibility** — Provide a unified view of infrastructure operations, permissions, policies, and deployment activities across projects and environments, improving governance at scale.
