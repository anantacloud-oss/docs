Modern infrastructure teams often face challenges that impact scalability, governance, security, and operational efficiency.

## Operational Challenges

- **Infrastructure Knowledge Silos** — Infrastructure expertise is often limited to a small group of specialists, creating bottlenecks and slowing down delivery.

- **Manual Infrastructure Requests** — Development teams frequently depend on platform engineers for provisioning, resulting in slower delivery and reduced agility.

- **Challenges Scaling Terraform Operations** — As infrastructure grows, managing deployments consistently across teams and environments becomes increasingly difficult.

## Governance & Security Challenges

- **Inconsistent Infrastructure Standards** — Different teams may follow different deployment practices, leading to configuration drift, security gaps, and operational complexity.

- **Governance and Compliance Challenges** — Managing approvals, access controls, and compliance requirements across multiple teams and environments can be difficult without centralized governance.

- **Complex Access Management** — Managing permissions across users, teams, and projects becomes increasingly challenging as organizations scale.

- **Credential Management Risks** — Distributing and maintaining cloud credentials securely across teams can introduce security risks and operational overhead.

## Visibility & Cost Challenges

- **Limited Cost Visibility** — Infrastructure costs are often discovered after deployment, making it difficult to predict and control cloud spending.

- **Limited Operational Visibility** — Tracking infrastructure activity, deployment status, and changes across environments can become difficult at scale.

## Platform & Tooling Challenges

- **Tool Sprawl** — Organizations frequently rely on multiple tools for provisioning, governance, identity management, and cost analysis, increasing operational complexity.

- **Disconnected Identity Systems** — Separate authentication and authorization systems can create inconsistent access management and user experiences.

- **Lack of Centralized Approvals** — Infrastructure changes may bypass consistent review processes, increasing governance and operational risks.
