# Challenges Axio Solves

Modern infrastructure teams often encounter operational challenges that impact scalability and productivity.

## Infrastructure Knowledge Silos

Infrastructure expertise is frequently concentrated within a small number of specialists.

This creates deployment bottlenecks and slows down delivery.

## Inconsistent Infrastructure Standards

Different teams may provision infrastructure differently, resulting in:

- Configuration drift
- Security inconsistencies
- Operational complexity

## Governance and Compliance Challenges

As organizations grow, controlling access, approvals, and policy enforcement becomes increasingly difficult.

## Limited Cost Visibility

Infrastructure costs are often discovered after deployment rather than before deployment.

## Tool Sprawl

Organizations commonly manage multiple systems for:

- Terraform execution
- Identity management
- Cost estimation
- Policy enforcement
- Access control

Axio reduces this operational overhead by centralizing these capabilities.
