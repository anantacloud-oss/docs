title: Challenges Axio Solves
---

Modern infrastructure teams often encounter operational challenges that impact scalability, governance, and operational efficiency as cloud environments continue to grow.

- **Infrastructure Knowledge Silos** — Infrastructure expertise is often concentrated within a small group of platform engineers or Terraform specialists. This dependency can create deployment bottlenecks, slow down project delivery, and make it difficult for development teams to provision infrastructure independently.

- **Inconsistent Infrastructure Standards** — Different teams often follow different deployment practices, configurations, and operational processes. This can lead to configuration drift, security inconsistencies, reduced standardization, and increased operational complexity across environments.

- **Governance and Compliance Challenges** — As organizations scale, enforcing consistent governance becomes increasingly complex. Managing approvals, access controls, security policies, and compliance requirements across multiple teams and environments can be difficult without centralized controls.

- **Limited Cost Visibility** — Infrastructure costs are frequently identified only after resources have been deployed. Without visibility into estimated costs during the deployment process, organizations may face budget overruns, inefficient resource utilization, and unexpected cloud spending.

- **Tool Sprawl** — Organizations commonly use multiple tools for Terraform execution, identity management, cost estimation, policy enforcement, and access control. Managing these disconnected systems can increase operational overhead and complexity. Axio simplifies infrastructure operations by centralizing these capabilities into a single platform.
