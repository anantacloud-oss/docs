# Why Axio?

Infrastructure teams today face increasing complexity as cloud environments expand.

Terraform provides a powerful foundation for Infrastructure as Code, but organizations often need additional capabilities around governance, access management, approvals, policy enforcement, and cost visibility.

Axio addresses these challenges through a unified platform experience.

## A Single Platform for Infrastructure Operations

Instead of combining multiple tools and custom workflows, Axio provides:

- Infrastructure automation
- Governance controls
- Access management
- Policy enforcement
- Cost estimation
- Identity integration
- Approval workflows

within a single platform.

## Enable Self-Service Without Sacrificing Control

Axio enables platform teams to standardize infrastructure delivery by managing reusable Terraform modules and approved deployment patterns. Development teams can provision infrastructure through a self-service experience without needing extensive Terraform knowledge or direct access to cloud accounts.

By simplifying infrastructure consumption while maintaining centralized governance, Axio helps organizations improve delivery speed, reduce operational dependencies, and ensure consistent, secure, and compliant infrastructure deployments across teams and environments.

## Deploy Infrastructure Without Writing Terraform

Axio allows platform teams to manage and maintain approved Terraform modules that represent standardized infrastructure patterns across the organization.

Instead of writing Terraform code, users simply select the appropriate module, provide the required input variables, and deploy infrastructure through a guided workflow. This approach reduces the Terraform expertise required to provision infrastructure while ensuring deployments follow organizational standards and best practices.

By abstracting Terraform complexity behind a self-service interface, Axio helps teams accelerate provisioning, reduce configuration errors, and enable broader adoption of Infrastructure as Code across the organization

## Simplify Platform Operations

Managing infrastructure often requires multiple tools for provisioning, governance, access management, approvals, and cost analysis. Coordinating these tools can increase operational complexity and create fragmented workflows.

Axio brings these capabilities together into a unified platform, helping teams streamline infrastructure operations, improve visibility, reduce manual processes, and manage deployments more efficiently across projects and environments.
