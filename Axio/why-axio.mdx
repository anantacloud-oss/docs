# Why Axio?

Infrastructure teams today face increasing complexity as cloud environments expand.

Terraform provides a powerful foundation for Infrastructure as Code, but organizations often need additional capabilities around governance, access management, approvals, policy enforcement, and cost visibility.

Axio addresses these challenges through a unified platform experience.

## A Single Platform for Infrastructure Operations

Instead of combining multiple tools and custom workflows, Axio provides:

- Infrastructure automation
- Governance controls
- Access management
- Policy enforcement
- Cost estimation
- Identity integration
- Approval workflows

within a single platform.

## Enable Self-Service Without Sacrificing Control

Axio enables platform teams to define and publish approved infrastructure patterns that can be reused across the organization. Developers can provision infrastructure through standardized workflows without requiring deep Terraform expertise or direct access to cloud accounts.

This approach accelerates infrastructure delivery, reduces dependency on platform teams for routine requests, and helps maintain security, governance, and operational standards across all environments.

## Simplify Platform Operations

Managing infrastructure often requires multiple tools for provisioning, governance, access management, approvals, and cost analysis. Coordinating these tools can increase operational complexity and create fragmented workflows.

Axio brings these capabilities together into a unified platform, helping teams streamline infrastructure operations, improve visibility, reduce manual processes, and manage deployments more efficiently across projects and environments.
