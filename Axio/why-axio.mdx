# Why Axio?

Infrastructure teams today face increasing complexity as cloud environments expand.

Terraform provides a powerful foundation for Infrastructure as Code, but organizations often need additional capabilities around governance, access management, approvals, policy enforcement, and cost visibility.

Axio addresses these challenges through a unified platform experience.

## A Single Platform for Infrastructure Operations

Instead of combining multiple tools and custom workflows, Axio provides:

- Infrastructure automation
- Governance controls
- Access management
- Policy enforcement
- Cost estimation
- Identity integration
- Approval workflows

within a single platform.

## Enable Self-Service Without Sacrificing Control

Platform teams can create approved infrastructure patterns while developers provision resources through controlled workflows.

This reduces operational bottlenecks while maintaining organizational standards.

## Simplify Platform Operations

Axio helps reduce the complexity associated with managing multiple infrastructure tools, integrations, and approval processes.
