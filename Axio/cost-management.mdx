Infrastructure decisions should consider both technical requirements and financial impact. Axio integrates cost visibility into infrastructure workflows, helping teams make informed deployment decisions before resources are provisioned.

## Cost Management Capabilities

- **Cost Estimation Before Deployment** — Understand the potential cost impact of infrastructure changes before resources are created. Early visibility helps teams avoid unexpected expenses and make better architectural decisions.

- **Budget Awareness** — Provide teams with greater awareness of infrastructure spending during the deployment process. This encourages more informed resource selection and promotes responsible cloud consumption.

- **Infracost Integration** — Integrate with Infracost profiles to estimate Terraform deployment costs directly within the platform. Cost estimates can be incorporated into deployment workflows, improving financial transparency.

- **Financial Governance** — Promote cost-conscious infrastructure practices across projects and teams. By making cost information accessible during deployment planning, organizations can align infrastructure decisions with business objectives.

- **Improved Visibility** — Give stakeholders greater insight into the financial impact of infrastructure changes. Enhanced visibility helps platform teams, developers, and management make more informed decisions.

- **Operational Efficiency** — Reduce the need for manual cost analysis by integrating cost estimation into existing infrastructure workflows, enabling faster and more efficient planning and deployment processes.
