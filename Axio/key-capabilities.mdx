title: Key Capabilities
---

- **Infrastructure Automation** — Centralized execution of Terraform workflows, enabling teams to provision, update, and manage infrastructure through a consistent and controlled deployment process.

- **Module Registry** — Publish, manage, and reuse standardized Terraform modules, helping teams accelerate deployments while maintaining infrastructure consistency.

- **Stack Management** — Deploy and manage infrastructure stacks across multiple environments using approved configurations and reusable deployment patterns.

- **Policy Management** — Enforce governance, security, and compliance requirements through centralized policy controls, ensuring infrastructure deployments align with organizational standards.

- **Role-Based Access Control** — Implement fine-grained access management through users, teams, roles, and organizations to ensure secure and controlled platform access.

- **Approval Workflows** — Introduce structured review and approval processes for infrastructure changes, helping reduce risk and improve operational governance.

- **Cost Visibility** — Gain insight into infrastructure costs before deployment through Infracost integration, enabling more informed planning and budgeting decisions.

- **Identity Integration** — Integrate with enterprise identity providers such as OIDC and Azure AD to simplify authentication, authorization, and user lifecycle management.
