# Key Capabilities

Axio provides capabilities designed to simplify infrastructure management and governance.

## Infrastructure Automation

Execute Terraform workflows through a centralized platform.

## Module Registry

Publish and manage reusable Terraform modules.

## Stack Management

Deploy and manage infrastructure stacks consistently across environments.

## Policy Management

Apply governance and compliance controls across infrastructure deployments.

## Role-Based Access Control

Manage permissions through users, teams, roles, and organizations.

## Approval Workflows

Introduce governance checkpoints before infrastructure changes are applied.

## Cost Visibility

Understand infrastructure costs before deployment through Infracost integration.

## Identity Integration

Integrate with enterprise identity providers using OIDC and Azure AD.
