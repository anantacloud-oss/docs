Axio provides capabilities designed to simplify infrastructure management, governance, and operational scalability across teams and environments.

## Infrastructure Management

- **Infrastructure Automation** — Centralized execution of Terraform workflows, enabling teams to provision, update, and manage infrastructure through a consistent and controlled deployment process.

- **Module Registry** — Manage reusable Terraform modules that represent approved infrastructure patterns. Platform teams can maintain standardized modules that can be consumed across projects and environments.

- **Self-Service Infrastructure** — Users can provision infrastructure by selecting approved Terraform modules and providing the required input variables. This reduces the need to write Terraform code while ensuring deployments follow organizational standards.

- **Stack Management** — Deploy and manage infrastructure stacks across multiple environments using approved configurations and reusable deployment patterns.

- **Infrastructure Lifecycle Management** — Manage infrastructure throughout its lifecycle, including provisioning, updates, monitoring, and controlled decommissioning.

## Governance & Security

- **Policy Management** — Enforce governance, security, and compliance requirements through centralized policy controls, ensuring infrastructure deployments align with organizational standards.

- **Role-Based Access Control** — Implement fine-grained access management through users, teams, roles, and organizations to ensure secure and controlled platform access.

- **Approval Workflows** — Introduce structured review and approval processes for infrastructure changes, helping reduce risk and improve operational governance.

- **Credential Management** — Securely manage cloud provider credentials and control how infrastructure deployments authenticate with target environments.

- **Infrastructure Governance** — Combine policies, approvals, access controls, and organizational standards to maintain consistent governance across infrastructure operations.

## Collaboration & Identity

- **Multi-Team Collaboration** — Organize users through teams, roles, and organizations to support collaborative infrastructure management across multiple projects and environments.

- **Identity Integration** — Integrate with enterprise identity providers such as OIDC and Azure AD to simplify authentication, authorization, and user lifecycle management.

## Cost & Operational Visibility

- **Cost Visibility** — Gain insight into infrastructure costs before deployment through Infracost integration, enabling more informed planning and budgeting decisions.

- **Operational Visibility** — Monitor infrastructure activities, deployment status, and execution workflows through a centralized platform interface.
