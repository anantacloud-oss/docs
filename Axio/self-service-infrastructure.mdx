---

One of Axio's primary goals is to enable self-service infrastructure delivery while maintaining governance, security, and operational standards. It allows development teams to provision infrastructure independently using approved and reusable deployment patterns.

## Self-Service Infrastructure Benefits

- **Empower Development Teams** — Developers can provision approved infrastructure through a simple and controlled interface without requiring deep Terraform expertise. This enables faster adoption of Infrastructure as Code practices across the organization.

- **Reduce Platform Team Bottlenecks** — Platform engineers can focus on building and governing reusable infrastructure standards while application teams independently consume approved infrastructure resources.

- **Standardized Deployments** — Infrastructure is deployed using reusable, validated, and governed modules, helping ensure consistency across projects, environments, and teams.

- **Faster Delivery** — Reduce manual provisioning requests, approval delays, and repetitive operational tasks, enabling teams to deliver infrastructure more quickly and efficiently.

- **Controlled Self-Service** — Teams gain the flexibility to provision infrastructure when needed while organizational policies, approvals, and access controls remain centrally managed.

- **Scalable Operations** — As infrastructure demand grows, self-service workflows help organizations support more teams and projects without proportionally increasing platform engineering overhead.
