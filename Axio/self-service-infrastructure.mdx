# Self-Service Infrastructure

One of Axio's primary goals is enabling self-service infrastructure delivery.

## Empower Development Teams

Developers can provision approved infrastructure without requiring Terraform expertise.

## Reduce Platform Team Bottlenecks

Platform engineers define standards while application teams consume approved infrastructure.

## Standardized Deployments

Infrastructure is deployed using reusable and governed modules.

## Faster Delivery

Reduce manual provisioning requests and operational delays.
