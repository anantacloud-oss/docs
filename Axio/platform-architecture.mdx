Axio combines infrastructure automation, governance controls, identity management, and deployment workflows into a unified platform. Its architecture is designed to help organizations standardize Infrastructure as Code operations while maintaining security, visibility, and scalability.

## Core Components

- **Registry** — Stores and manages reusable infrastructure services and Terraform modules. This enables teams to standardize deployments, reduce duplication, and accelerate infrastructure provisioning across projects.

- **Projects** — Organize infrastructure resources, environments, and teams within logical boundaries. Projects help simplify ownership, access management, and operational visibility.

- **Stacks** — Represent deployable infrastructure configurations built from approved modules and variables. Stacks provide a consistent way to manage infrastructure across different environments.

- **Credentials** — Securely manage cloud provider authentication and access for deployments. Centralized credential management helps protect sensitive information while simplifying operations.

- **Policies** — Enforce governance, security, and compliance requirements across infrastructure operations. Policies help ensure deployments align with organizational standards and best practices.

- **Roles and Teams** — Manage permissions and access through role-based access control. This ensures users have the appropriate level of access based on their responsibilities.

- **VCS Integration** — Connect GitHub repositories for module management and version control. This enables infrastructure code to remain aligned with existing development workflows.

- **Cost Management** — Provide infrastructure cost estimation and financial visibility before deployment. Early cost awareness helps teams make informed infrastructure decisions and avoid unexpected spending.

- **Approval Workflows** — Introduce review and approval processes for controlled infrastructure changes. This adds governance checkpoints and reduces the risk of unintended deployments.

- **Identity Integration** — Support centralized authentication through OIDC and Azure AD. Integration with enterprise identity providers simplifies user management and strengthens security.

- **Infrastructure Lifecycle** — Manage infrastructure through provisioning, approvals, deployment, monitoring, and ongoing operations. A structured lifecycle helps ensure consistency, governance, and operational control.
